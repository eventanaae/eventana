EVENTANA01  Introduction

Hello there…

Events that belong here.

Eventana Events is an Emirati woman-led event design and production company, established in 2021 and based in Dubai.

We plan, design and deliver events across the UAE — for private companies, government organisations, schools and private clients. Over the past five years we have built our reputation on a simple standard: the event opens on time, looks exactly as approved, and reflects the people it was made for.

We are not a booking agency. We design the concept, produce the setup, own the equipment, train the team, and run the event on the day. One company, from first brief to final handover.

EVENTANA02  About Eventana

About us

Built in the UAE, for the UAE.

Eventana was founded in 2021 by an Emirati businesswoman who saw the same gap at event after event: productions that were technically competent but culturally hollow — designs that could have been dropped into any city in the world.

Five years on, Eventana is a full-service production house with its own warehouse, its own equipment and its own trained crews, serving clients in every emirate.

EVENTANA03  What Makes Us Different

Our advantage

We understand where we are.

Most event companies in the UAE deliver a global template. We deliver an event that belongs to this country.

Being Emirati-led is not a line on our licence — it is how we read a brief. We know what a majlis-style welcome signals to senior guests. We know how to design for Ramadan and Eid without reducing them to decoration. We know which gestures read as generous here, and which read as careless. And we know how to bring all of it into a design that feels current, elegant and premium — not traditional for the sake of it.

Cultural fluency, by default

Protocol, seating, hosting order, timing around prayer, the tone of a welcome. Our clients do not have to explain these things to us, or check them afterwards.

Heritage, made modern

Emirati motifs, palettes and hospitality traditions interpreted through a clean, contemporary design language.

Fully bilingual

Arabic and English across our team, our design work and our client communication — with Arabic treated as a first language, not a translation.

The right instinct for the room

Government protocol, a corporate client’s brand governance and a family celebration each demand a different register. We read the room before we design for it.

EVENTANA05  Our Experience

Trusted by private and government organisations across the UAE.

Since 2021 we have delivered events for private companies and government organisations across the Emirates, alongside schools, community bodies and private clients.

That mix matters. A government occasion demands protocol and cultural precision. A corporate client demands brand governance, security clearance and a delivery record that survives scrutiny. A family celebration demands warmth. Each is a different discipline — and we have built the company to hold all three.

Arrive early, always

Institutional venues have access windows, security procedures and facilities teams. We plan around them rather than discover them.

Approve before producing

Nothing is built, printed or installed before it is signed off. At this level, a surprise is a failure.

Design to the brief, not to a template

Corporate work is produced to the client’s brand guidelines. Government occasions are designed to the standard the occasion deserves.

Leave nothing behind

Full dismantle, full site clearance, photographic handover. Facilities teams remember who does this properly.

EVENTANA07  How We Work

Brief to handover

How we work.

01

Brief

We meet, and we listen. Occasion, audience, venue, attendance, protocol requirements, brand guidelines, budget.

02

Design

We return a full visual concept with the recommended service mix, layout and a written scope. Nothing is produced before you approve it.

03

Plan

Date secured, equipment reserved against it, delivery and setup windows agreed with your venue and facilities team.

04

Produce

Custom theming, branded items and printed personalisation enter production with lead time built in.

05

Deliver

Our crew install ahead of guest arrival. Attendants run the stations, entertainers run their programmes, and one supervisor holds the schedule as your single point of contact.

06

Handover

We dismantle, clear the site and return the venue as we found it — followed by a photographic handover.

Why organisations stay

Six reasons clients stay with us.

EVENTANA09  Safety & Assurance

The standards behind the event.

Children are a primary audience at many of our events, and our procedures reflect that.

Equipment inspected before every deployment, with anchoring appropriate to the surface and conditions.

Capacity limits enforced per ride, with an Eventana supervisor present at active-play equipment throughout.

Food stations operated exclusively by trained Eventana attendants — never by guests or children.

Child-safe consumables and age-appropriate materials across all activities.

Uniformed, briefed and identifiable crew on site for the full duration of the event.

Venue protection and complete site clearance as standard, not as an extra.

Written scope, confirmed timings and a photographic handover on completion.
