## ⚠️ The one real tension — please read this before designing

**Your brand is playful. This audience is not.**

Eventana's visual identity is pastel, rounded, multicoloured and warm. It is genuinely lovely, and it is exactly right for parents booking a birthday. But a profile going to corporate and government organisations is read by procurement and marketing teams who are scanning for *credibility*. The full playful treatment — six colours a page, bubble lettering, scattered confetti shapes — will read as a children's party supplier, and it will quietly cost you the meeting.

**Do not abandon the brand. Restrain it.** The profile should be recognisably Eventana, applied with discipline:

| Keep | Restrain |
|---|---|
| Cream background and charcoal text | No full-page colour fills |
| The Eventana wordmark, at full colour | Use it once per page, small, not oversized |
| Rounded corners on photos and panels | Reduce the radius slightly — softer, not bubbly |
| The lavender/mint/blush tile system | **Two accent colours per page maximum** |
| Coral + teal headline pairing | Set headlines in the rounded display font but **larger, with more letter-spacing and far more white space** |
| The script font | **One phrase per page maximum** — and never on the experience or safety pages |
| Flat line icons | Keep them minimal and single-colour on corporate pages |

**Three pages should be almost entirely restrained** — Our Experience, Safety & Assurance, and Contact. Cream, ink, one accent line. Let the content carry them. The contrast will make the gallery pages feel more vivid, not less.

Think: *the same brand, dressed for a board meeting.*

## We understand where we are.

Most event companies in the UAE deliver a global template. We deliver an event that belongs to this country.

Being Emirati-led is not a line on our licence — it is how we read a brief. We know what a majlis-style welcome signals to senior guests. We know how a National Day event differs from a Union Day event, and why that matters. We know how to design for Ramadan and Eid without reducing them to decoration. We know when a gathering should separate hosting for families, and when it should not. We know which gestures read as generous here, and which read as careless.

And we know how to bring all of it into a design that feels current, elegant and premium — not traditional for the sake of it.

**Where this shows up**

**Cultural fluency, by default.** Protocol, seating, hosting order, timing around prayer, the tone of a welcome. Our clients do not have to explain these things to us, or check them afterwards.

**Fully bilingual.** Arabic and English across our team, our design work and our client communication — with Arabic treated as a first language, not a translation.

**The right instinct for the room.** Government protocol, a bank's brand governance and a family celebration each demand a different register. We read the room before we design for it.

This local understanding is the single clearest reason clients stay with us — and the reason we are trusted with events that carry an organisation's name.

# PAGE 07 — OUR EXPERIENCE

## Trusted by private and government organisations across the UAE.

Since 2021 we have delivered events for private companies and government organisations across the Emirates, alongside schools, community bodies and private clients.

That mix matters. A government occasion demands protocol and cultural precision. A corporate client demands brand governance, security clearance and a delivery record that survives scrutiny. A family celebration demands warmth. Each is a different discipline — and we have built the company to hold all three.

**What working at this level has taught us**

**Arrive early, always.** Institutional venues have access windows, security procedures and facilities teams. We plan around them rather than discover them.

**Approve before producing.** Nothing is built, printed or installed before it is signed off. At this level, a surprise is a failure.

**Design to the brief, not to a template.** Corporate work is produced to the client's brand guidelines. Government occasions are designed to the standard the occasion deserves.

**Leave nothing behind.** Full dismantle, full site clearance, photographic handover. Facilities teams remember who does this properly.
