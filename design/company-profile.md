# 2. About Eventana

Eventana Events is a UAE-based event production company specialising in the design, build and on-site delivery of family, children's and community events.

We are an **end-to-end operator, not a broker**. The backdrops, inflatables, food stations, games and machines that appear at your event are owned by Eventana and maintained in our own warehouse. They are transported, installed, staffed and dismantled by our own uniformed crew. Nothing is sub-contracted to a third party we cannot vouch for — which is why we can commit to a setup time and hold to it.

That model was built for the private celebrations market, where a birthday cannot be postponed and a two-hour delay is not recoverable. Applied to corporate work — a family day, a mall activation, a school festival, a staff appreciation event — the same discipline produces something clients tell us is unusual in this market: **an event that starts on schedule, looks exactly like the approved design, and leaves the venue as it was found.**

# 3. Vision, Mission & Values

### Vision
To be the UAE's most trusted name in family and children's event production — the company organisations call when the event has to be right the first time.

### Mission
To take the entire weight of an event off our client's shoulders — design, equipment, logistics, staffing, safety and teardown — and return it as a single, transparent, professionally delivered experience.

**Reliability before creativity.** A beautiful concept delivered late is a failed event. We schedule backwards from the guests' arrival time and build in the buffer.

**Respect for the venue.** We arrive, protect surfaces, install, deliver, dismantle and clean. Facilities teams remember the companies that do this.

# 4. What We Do — Service Portfolio

Eventana operates nine in-house service lines. Any combination can be assembled into a single event, on a single contract, with a single point of contact.

### 4.1 Theming, Backdrops & Décor
Custom-designed backdrop walls in three scales, balloon installations in specified brand or theme colours, character cut-outs, welcome signage, cake and display staging, and fully styled seating — themed placemats, centrepieces, custom-labelled bottles and tableware. Designs are produced to the client's theme or brand guidelines and approved visually before production.

### 4.2 Food & Beverage Stations
Attended stations, each staffed by an Eventana service attendant — **guests are never left to operate equipment**. Available lines include popcorn, cotton candy, slush, corn, ice cream, hot dogs, mini pancakes, nachos, burger sliders, chocolate fountain and hot chocolate. Standard station capacity is four hours of continuous service for up to 40 guests, with additional serving volumes available for larger crowds.

### 4.3 Inflatables & Active Play
Bouncy castles in three colourways, bubble house, ball-pool slide, water slides for summer and outdoor programmes, and a 12×6 m slippery football pitch. All inflatables are supervised, capacity-limited per ride, and installed with safety anchoring appropriate to the surface. Socks are required and can be supplied for all attendees.

### 4.4 Carnival Games
Attended skill-and-prize games — target throw, can smash, basketball hoop and bowling — run by our crew across the event duration, with prizes included. Ideal for footfall-driven activations and family days where guests circulate.

### 4.5 Special-Effect Machines
Foam, bubble and snow machines for atmosphere and photo moments, deployed indoors or outdoors according to the venue assessment.

### 4.6 Entertainment & Performers
Professional acrobatic performers and stilt walkers, balloon-twisting artists, face painters using kid-safe products, and costumed mascot characters for guest welcome and photo opportunities. Performer numbers are scaled to expected attendance.

### 4.7 Creative Activity Workshops
Structured, supervised, materials-included workshops: slime making, cupcake and cookie decorating, canvas and pottery painting, T-shirt, tote and hat painting, bracelet and doll making, notebook and mirror decorating, and floral arrangement. Each session runs approximately two hours with Eventana assistants and all supplies provided — a strong fit for school programmes and corporate family days where children need occupied, and photographable, time.

### 4.8 Branded Giveaways & Personalised Items
Custom-printed T-shirts, party hats, face banners, VIP wristbands and bespoke digital artwork — produced to the client's logo, artwork or a commissioned illustration. Made-to-order items require production lead time and are quoted separately.

### 4.9 Digital Design & Invitations
Themed digital invitations as static images or animated video, branded event artwork, and personalised illustration — delivered as ready-to-share digital files.

# 7. How We Work

**1 · Brief.** We capture the occasion, date, venue, expected attendance, age profile and budget envelope. For corporate work this is a scoping call, not a form.

**2 · Design proposal.** We return a themed concept with the recommended service mix, layout logic and a full written scope.

**3 · Approval.** You approve the design and scope in writing. Custom artwork, brand-matched theming and personalised items are visually approved before production begins.

**4 · Confirmation & scheduling.** The date is secured and equipment is reserved against it. Delivery, setup, event and dismantle windows are locked and shared with your venue or facilities team.

**5 · Production.** Made-to-order items — branded giveaways, printed personalisation, custom illustration — enter production with lead time built in.

**6 · Setup.** Crew arrive within the agreed window ahead of guest arrival. Every element is installed, tested and photographed before we stand back.

**7 · Delivery.** Attendants operate the food stations. Entertainers and workshop assistants run their programmes. A supervisor holds the schedule and is your single point of contact throughout.

**8 · Teardown & handover.** We dismantle, load out and clear the site. The venue is returned to its original condition.

**Lead times.** Standard bookings are confirmed comfortably ahead of the event date; urgent bookings inside 72 hours are accepted subject to equipment and crew availability, with a rush surcharge, and cannot be accepted inside 24 hours. Made-to-order personalised items require approximately two weeks. **Corporate events are best briefed [N] weeks ahead** [confirm your preferred corporate lead time].

# 8. Why Eventana

**We own the inventory.** Backdrops, inflatables, carts, machines and games are Eventana assets, maintained in our own warehouse and tracked against every booking. We do not promise equipment we then have to go and rent.

**We staff every station.** Attended food stations, supervised inflatables, crewed games. Guests enjoy the event; our people run it.

**One contract, one contact.** A twelve-element event is one scope, one quotation, one account manager and one crew — not six vendors converging on your venue with six arrival times.

**Purpose-built operating technology.** Eventana runs on its own booking and operations platform: real-time equipment availability, so a date is never double-committed; a live crew dispatch and event calendar; and automated client updates from confirmation through to setup. **This is why we can commit to timings.** Availability is not a phone call to a warehouse — it is a system state.

**Nationwide coverage.** All seven emirates plus Al Ain, served from our own fleet and logistics schedule.
