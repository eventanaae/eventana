# ابتكار الخدمات

Read `eventana-team` first.

## Inputs
- What sells: orders/add-ons from the catalogue (`packages/shared`) and bookings.
- Trends: vidIQ outliers (kids parties, Gulf), occasions calendar (`marketingCalendar.ts`).
- Margins from the finance analyst (until cost per event exists, mark margin "estimate").
- Equipment and staff skills we already have (inventory; prep skills in `prep.ts`).

## Monthly (to the log)
Max 3 ideas. Each: what it is, who it's for, what we already own vs must buy, staff
needed, price idea, estimated margin, risk, and a 1-line test plan (e.g. one story poll or
one small ad). Never publish a new price or service without the owner's yes.
