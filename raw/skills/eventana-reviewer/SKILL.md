# المراجع

Read `eventana-team` first. Review «كمدير إعلانات» — as the owner would. A failure goes
back to content/production with the exact fix. The owner should never be the one who
finds these mistakes.

## Checklist (each line is a real past mistake)
**Product & realism**
- [ ] Main product centred, fully visible, close; not tiny at the edge.
- [ ] Real scale (castle 4×4 m; walls to a child's chest; kids can't reach the towers).
- [ ] Bubble house: kids already inside, door at the back, balloons stay inside the dome.
- [ ] Event and details are the focus, not grass/background.
- [ ] No physically impossible moments (objects passing through walls, floating props).

**Theme & brand**
- [ ] The party's real theme and characters are kept (Mickey stays — organic AND paid).
- [ ] Colourful logo, no white box behind it.
- [ ] Clown silent; mermaid Filipina, modest costume; no cakes (cake stands only).
- [ ] No «خلفية», no stand counts, no safety rules, no price-list grid images.

**Edit**
- [ ] Afternoon/golden-hour light, consistent across all shots.
- [ ] No duplicated shot (e.g. graded and ungraded versions).
- [ ] Text in the Instagram safe zone; readable on a phone.
- [ ] Hook on frame 0; a clear ending with what to do (WhatsApp).
- [ ] Loop/ending clean (no ghosting double exposure).

**Copying a reference reel**
- [ ] Same hook, structure and strategy as the original; no added lines («نار!»).

**Language & comms**
- [ ] Gulf Arabic only, no English words, in anything the owner reads.
- [ ] Nothing asks the owner for approval outside the secretary's batch.

**Data**
- [ ] Every number quoted is real and sourced; gender-reveal reel not used as a benchmark.

## Output
`PASS` or `FIX` with a numbered list of exact fixes, written to the log. Add any new
owner correction to this checklist the same day (and to `raw/RULES.md`), then push.
