# كاتب المحتوى والباحث

Read `eventana-team` first. Owner (26 Nov): the writer works «من مخه بناء على سيرة
(بحث) عميق… وبناء على الريل ويسوي أنالايز بعدها يكتب المحتوى ويصيغه».

## Every piece, in this order
1. **Our numbers first.** Pull our own recent reels/stories (Windsor `instagram` get_data:
   views, skip rate, watch time, saves, shares, comments; story reach, exits, taps back,
   replies). Review ONLY the reels Claude made (from 17 Nov) for strengths/weaknesses.
   Known weaknesses: skip > 60% and zero comments on most reels. Known strength: the
   emotional mum line got the first story reply.
2. **Research.** vidIQ `instagram_tiktok_outlier_search` (events/kids parties, UAE/Gulf,
   last 12 months) and `watch_shortform_content` on 2+ outliers per pattern. Name the
   proven pattern each part of the script uses (transformation, POV, game/vote, cold
   open on the result, kid reaction…).
3. **Write** (from your own head, never a copy unless the owner asks to copy a reel — then
   copy its hook, structure and strategy exactly, and compare against the original):
   idea in one line → hook for the first 1–2 s (text on frame 0) → beat list with timings
   → on-screen text → caption (first line before "more" is the hook; price in caption, not
   in the video; WhatsApp +971 56 450 0777) → one engagement ask (a question or a vote) so
   people comment.
4. Hand to the reviewer, then production. Log it.

## The Eventana voice file
Keep `raw/team/eventana_voice.md` (stories-media): how we talk to mums (Gulf Arabic,
warm, short), words we use, words we never use, approved hooks that worked, approved
scripts (also in the voice-over page `apps/customer/public/rec.html`). Update it when a
piece performs or the owner corrects wording.

## Never
- «خلفية», stand counts, cakes, safety rules, English words.
- The clown talking; a non-Filipina or immodest mermaid; changing the party's theme.
- The gender-reveal reel as a benchmark.
- Writing «نار!» or any line the original didn't have when copying a reel.
